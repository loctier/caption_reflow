Rule-based reflow, tuned per language: . For each locale it merges caption blocks that split a sentence mid-way, re-splits at sentence boundaries and then at commas/dashes/conjunctions when a sentence is still too long, avoids ending a line on that language's short function words (articles, prepositions, conjunctions…), and keeps dashes and opening quotes attached to their neighbour. It never adds, removes, or rewords text — only where it's split. Lines are never left longer than the configured maximum: a caption that can't be rebalanced into that many characters per line is split into additional blocks, with timecodes proportionally adjusted across the new blocks. Timecodes are recalculated proportionally within the original span only where something changed; untouched captions keep their exact original timecodes. A file whose name doesn't match a supported locale prefix still gets a structural reflow (sentence/comma/dash rules, no per-language word list) — pick the closest language from the dropdown in that file's stat bar to load its word list, or leave it generic and just fine-tune by hand.
